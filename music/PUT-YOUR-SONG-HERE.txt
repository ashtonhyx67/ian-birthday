BACKGROUND MUSIC — TWO SONGS
============================

The site plays one song everywhere, and a second song on the picture page.
Walking onto page three fades one out and the other in; walking off fades
back, and the first song carries on from where it was.

  song.mp3     the main song, heard on every page      <- already here
  photos.mp3   the picture page's own song             <- drop yours in here

To add the picture-page song:

  1. Put the audio file in this folder.
  2. Rename it to        photos.mp3
     (or keep its own name and change the "musicPhotos:" line near the top
      of index.html to match)
  3. Refresh the page.

mp3, m4a, ogg and wav all work.

If photos.mp3 is missing, nothing switches — the main song simply plays
through the picture page as well. If song.mp3 is missing too, the page falls
back to a soft acoustic guitar loop it generates itself. It is never silent.

Volume for both lives on the "musicVolume:" line in index.html (0 to 1).
